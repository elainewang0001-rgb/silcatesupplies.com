Product Overview

Our Silicon-Based Flame Retardant is the world's top-selling silicon-based FR by market share — a designation earned through 71 national patents, over a decade of continuous R&D, and successful deployment across 30+ countries. It is the defining product of Shandong Zhongzhi Chemical Technology and holds the distinction of China's "National Manufacturing Single Champion Product."

Unlike halogenated flame retardants that release toxic HBr or HCl gases during combustion, our silicon-based system achieves flame retardancy through a fundamentally clean chemistry: silicon-oxygen bonds form a protective char layer that blocks heat transfer and oxygen supply. The result is a high-LOI, anti-dripping, low-smoke FR that meets the most demanding global regulatory standards.

The product is available in two forms — a water-soluble liquid for textile finishing and liquid coating applications, and a solid powder for polymer compounding and dry-process applications. Both forms deliver equivalent FR performance and share the same core silicon chemistry.

Key Features

  • Halogen-Free: No chlorine, no bromine. Fully RoHS/REACH compliant. No HBr/HCl off-gassing on combustion.
  • LOI ≥ 29%: Limiting oxygen index exceeds 29% for textile substrates. UL94 V-0 achievable in engineering plastics.
  • Anti-Dripping: Suppresses melt-drip behavior in thermoplastics and synthetic textiles, reducing fire spread.
  • Smoke Suppression: Significantly lower smoke density vs. conventional FR systems. Critical for enclosed-space safety compliance.
  • Non-Toxic: Heavy metals ≤ 5 mg/kg. Polybrominated biphenyls ≤ 5 mg/kg. Safe for consumer and industrial applications.
  • Dual Form: Water-soluble liquid (for textiles, coatings) or solid powder (for plastics compounding, cables, construction).
  • Synergy-Compatible: Works synergistically with ATH, phosphate, and intumescent systems for enhanced performance.

Available Forms

💧 Water-Soluble Liquid

Designed for textile finishing (pad-dry-cure), FR coating formulations, and paper treatment. Easy spray or exhaust application. Ideal for cotton, linen, polyester-cotton blends. Compatible with water-based binder systems at pH 6–10.

🧪 Solid Powder

Designed for polymer compounding in PP, PE, PA, PVC, and engineering plastics. Directly blendable on twin-screw extruder. Used in wire & cable insulation, electronics housings, automotive components, and construction materials.
